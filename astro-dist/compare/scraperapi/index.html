Frequently asked questions

Is dataslayer a ScraperAPI alternative?+

For social-media data, yes. ScraperAPI returns page content and leaves parsing to you; dataslayer returns typed records from public social platforms so there is nothing to parse.

Do I still need a proxy with dataslayer?+

No. Proxying and fetching are handled inside each scraper on Apify. You send one request per run.

How is dataslayer priced?+

Per result returned, at each scraper's published rate, through your Apify account. No subscription and no seat minimum.
