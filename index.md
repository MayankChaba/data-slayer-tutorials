Practical, copy-paste tutorials for scraping and automating social platforms with
[Apify](https://apify.com) actors — no login required, no proxies to manage.

Every tutorial is a real, runnable recipe: the input you send, the output you get,
and the actor that does the work.

## What you can do here

- **Scrape LinkedIn** — company posts, profiles, jobs, and engagement data.
- **Scrape Instagram** — posts, reels, audio/trending sounds, and creator leads.
- **Scrape Facebook, TikTok & X** — posts, comments, ads, and audience data.
- **Automate it** — wire any actor into n8n, Make, or Zapier with our templates.

## Tutorials

Tutorials are being published here. Each one targets a specific job to be done:

- Scrape LinkedIn company posts without cookies
- Pull Instagram post & reel analytics by URL
- Refresh a CRM from LinkedIn profile URLs
- …and more
